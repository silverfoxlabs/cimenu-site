Privacy Policy

Last updated August 20, 2026

CIMenu is a local-only macOS app. It talks to two external services — Apple's App Store Connect API, and, only if you turn it on, a third-party AI provider for optional AI-powered failure analysis — and nothing else. There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, and no telemetry of any kind in CIMenu.

Credentials

Your App Store Connect API key (Issuer ID, Key ID, and private key) is stored only in the macOS Keychain on your Mac, protected so it's readable only after your Mac has been unlocked at least once since restart. It is never synced to iCloud, never written to a log file, and never transmitted anywhere except directly to Apple's App Store Connect API to authenticate your requests.

If you optionally configure a third-party AI provider (BYOK — "bring your own key"), that provider's API key is stored the same way, in the Keychain, kept separate from your App Store Connect credentials.

Build data

Xcode Cloud build logs are fetched from App Store Connect only when you open a failed build or ask a question about a build, and are not saved to disk beyond cached build metadata (status, branch, commit — not log text).

AI analysis

CIMenu's failure analysis runs in layers, and only the last one ever leaves your Mac:

  • Log parsing — pattern matching that runs entirely on-device. Nothing is transmitted.
  • On-device AI summary — uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework, when available on your Mac. Your build log never leaves your device for this step.
  • Optional third-party AI (BYOK) — off by default, and only used if you explicitly enable it and configure your own API key. Even then, only a structured summary of the failure (error type, file, line) is sent — never your raw build log — unless you separately opt in to sharing a log excerpt for follow-up questions.

Network access

CIMenu's outbound network access is limited to Apple's App Store Connect API, and, only if you've enabled it, your configured AI provider's endpoint. CIMenu runs inside Apple's App Sandbox.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash-reporting SDK
  • No advertising or tracking
  • No selling or sharing your data with third parties
  • No account required to use CIMenu beyond your own App Store Connect credentials
